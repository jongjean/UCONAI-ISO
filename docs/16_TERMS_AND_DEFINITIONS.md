# 16. Terms and Definitions

Date: 2026-05-03
Status: Validation policy baseline

## Objective

Terms, definitions, symbols and abbreviated terms are high-impact elements. They require careful reuse checks, consistency checks, and change control.

## Controls

| Control | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Existing term check | Avoid conflicting definitions |
| First-use check | Keep abbreviations coherent |
| Deprecated term flag | Warn against obsolete language |
| Clause 3 generator | Produce structured terms section |

## Completion Criteria

- Term entries are structured.
- Definitions can cite source or project rationale.
- AI proposes changes without silently rewriting approved definitions.
